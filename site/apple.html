Apple interview problems, by pattern

All 69 problems from Apple's six-month LeetCode list (7 Easy, 53 Medium, 9 Hard), grouped by the pattern that solves them. 67 are solved on this site: open one to see which pattern to choose and why, the approach, the code with a line-by-line explanation, and a player that steps through the code on LeetCode's own example. Easy warm-ups and SQL/JavaScript problems link to LeetCode.

On the pattern pages these problems carry a Apple badge. Frequency is LeetCode's relative score for this list (100 = asked most often). Read a pattern page first, then work through its group here from the most to the least frequent problem.

A 6-month plan

About 5–6 problems on 5 days a week. Each month: read the pattern page, solve its worked examples without looking, then this list. Re-solve anything you needed a hint for one week later.
